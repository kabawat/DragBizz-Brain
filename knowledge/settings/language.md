# App Language Settings

## Overview

DragBizz app language is set at **Settings → Language** (`/dashboard/settings?tab=language`). There are **4 language options**. Changing language updates the whole UI immediately and also sets the voice assistant speech language. Product names and other data you typed stay in the language you entered them.

Staff and owners can both change language. It is a **per-user, per-device** preference (remembered ~1 year on that browser/device) — it does not change language for other staff or other computers.

## Language options (4)

| Option | Display name | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| English | English | Default for many installs |
| Hindi | हिंदी | Full Hindi UI |
| Gujarati | ગુજરાતી | Full Gujarati UI |
| Hinglish | Hinglish | Hindi + English mix UI |

There are **no other** app languages today (no Marathi, Tamil, etc.).

## How to change language

1. Open **Settings** (`/dashboard/settings`).
2. Open the **Language** tab.
3. Click one of the four cards: **English / हिंदी / ગુજરાતી / Hinglish**.
4. The UI switches immediately — no save button, no restart needed.

The currently selected language shows a check mark. A short note on the page reminds you that your own data (product names, etc.) stays as typed.

## Business rules

- Four options only: English, Hindi, Gujarati, Hinglish.
- Applies to menus, buttons, settings labels, and the voice assistant (`Alt+V`) speech language.
- Does **not** translate invoices, product names, customer names, or other business data you entered.
- Does **not** sync to other devices or other staff accounts — each person sets language on each device.
- The Account tab may also show a language control; the dedicated **Language** tab is the primary place to change it.

## FAQ

**Q: How many languages does DragBizz support?**  
**4** — English, Hindi (हिंदी), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), and Hinglish.

**Q: How do I change the app language?**  
Settings → Language → click the language card you want. It applies instantly.

**Q: Do I need to save or restart?**  
No — the change applies immediately.

**Q: Does changing language affect my staff or another computer?**  
No — language is per user and per device.

**Q: Will my invoices / product names become Hindi?**  
No — only the app UI language changes. Your entered data stays as typed.
