# Getting Started: Account, Agency & First Store

## Overview

A new DragBizz account is ready in three quick steps: **register (with OTP)** → **create your agency** (your business identity with its own subdomain) → **create your first store**. After that you land on the dashboard with the free **Khata Free** plan already active — no payment or card needed.

## Workflow

1. **Register** — enter first/last name, email **or** phone, and a password (minimum 8 characters with a letter and a number). A **5-digit OTP** (valid 10 minutes) arrives on your email/phone; verify it — "Account created successfully! Welcome to DragBizz."
2. **Create agency** — Agency Name and a **subdomain** (lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens — becomes `yourname.dragbizz.com`). One agency per account.
3. **Create first store** — a 3-step wizard: Basic (Store Name and Phone required; GST/PAN optional with format verification) → Address (optional, skippable) → Business category (required).
4. You're in — the dashboard opens with the Khata Free plan: unlimited customers/khata/cashbook/reminders, 30 draft invoices per day, 50 products, 1 store.

## Business rules

- One account per email/phone — "An account with this email/phone already exists. Please login instead."
- The agency is created once; its subdomain can't easily change later — choose carefully.
- GSTIN entered on the store is validated (and can be verified/auto-filled); it also unlocks GST features later.
- Everything is upgradeable later — plans, stores, staff — the free tier has no time pressure (it doesn't expire).

## Examples

- "Sirf khata ke liye account banana hai" → register → agency → store → done, free forever for khata use.
- "GST number baad mein daal sakte hain?" → yes — add it anytime in Settings → Store.

## Errors

- OTP: "Invalid verification code…", "Verification code has expired…", 60-second resend cooldown.
- "Please enter a valid email address or phone number."
- Subdomain taken / invalid format (lowercase letters, digits, hyphens only).
- "Store name is required" / "Phone number is required" / "Store category is required".

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between agency and store?** 
The agency is your business account (one per login); stores are physical/online outlets under it. Plans and billing are per agency; most daily work is per store.

**Q: Do I need a GST number to start?** 
No — GST is optional. Without it, GST analytics/exports stay hidden; everything else works.

**Q: Can I add more stores later?** 
Yes, up to your plan's store limit (free = 1, Standard = 2, Premium = 5).
