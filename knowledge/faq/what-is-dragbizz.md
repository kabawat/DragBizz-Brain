# What is DragBizz?

## Overview

**DragBizz** is a store / SME business app for Indian shops: billing, khata (udhari), stock, GST, suppliers, cashbook, staff, and reports — in one dashboard. Start free on **Khata Free**; unlock POS, release invoices, FIFO inventory, and full reports on paid plans. Home: `/dashboard`. Plans: `/dashboard/management/subscription`.

## What you can do

| Area | What it covers | Open |
|---|---|---|
| Invoices & POS | Draft → release bills, thermal print, payments | `/dashboard/invoices` · **Alt+I** · POS **Alt+K** |
| Customers & Khata | Customers, udhari ledger, reminders | `/dashboard/customers` · **Alt+C** |
| Products & Stock | Catalog, FIFO stock, low-stock | `/dashboard/products` · **Alt+P** · Stock **Alt+S** |
| Suppliers & POs | Suppliers, purchase orders, bills | `/dashboard/suppliers` · **Alt+U** |
| GST & Tally | GST analytics, CA export, Tally XML | `/dashboard/analytics` · Tally `/dashboard/integrations/tally` |
| Cashbook & Expenses | Daily cash in/out, expense tracking | `/dashboard/cashbook` · **Alt+B** |

## Why shops use DragBizz

- **One place for the counter** — bill, collect payment, update khata and stock without jumping apps.
- **Built for Indian retail** — GST, khata/udhari, UPI, thermal print, Hindi/Gujarati UI.
- **Works with your CA** — export for CA and **Tally Prime** XML (Integrations → Tally).
- **Start free** — Khata Free: unlimited customers/khata/cashbook; upgrade when you need POS / release / full ERP.
- **Keyboard-fast** — **Alt+letter** navigation, **Shift+N** create, **Ctrl+S** / **F1** save. Full list: Support → Shortcuts (**Shift+?**).

## DragBizz vs Tally

| | **DragBizz** | **Tally** |
|---|---|---|
| Main job | Day-to-day store ops (billing, stock, khata, POS) | Accounting books & financials |
| Who uses it | Shop owner / staff at the counter | CA / accountant |
| How they connect | Export vouchers as **Tally XML** from GST Analytics → Import in Tally Prime | Imports DragBizz XML |

Use DragBizz to run the shop; use Tally (or your CA) for full books. Setup: `/dashboard/integrations/tally`.

## DragBizz vs other ERPs (e.g. Zoho)

DragBizz is aimed at **Indian small/medium shops** that need khata + GST + fast billing (+ optional Tally handoff). Prefer DragBizz when you want:

- Counter-first billing / POS and udhari khata
- Simple GST analytics + CA / Tally export
- Free khata tier to start, then upgrade modules

For deep multi-country ERP / accounting suites, other products may fit better — for store ops in DragBizz’s modules, stick to DragBizz docs and your plan at `/dashboard/management/subscription`.

## FAQ

**Q: Is DragBizz only accounting software?**  
No — it is store ops (billing, stock, customers). Accounting depth is via CA/Tally export.

**Q: Do I need Tally to use DragBizz?**  
No. Tally export is optional for shops whose CA uses Tally Prime.
